Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FF Tournament collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access our Free Fire tournament, coin, referral, wallet, payout, and rewarded ads features.

1. App Owner

FF Tournament is operated by Anup Naik. For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or support, contact us at anupsnaik7349@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and store the following information:

  • Name, email address, password authentication status, and mobile or WhatsApp number
  • Free Fire UID and tournament join details
  • Tournament details created by the organizer, including room ID, room password, match time, entry coins, and prize information
  • Coin balance, rewarded ad coin history, tournament entry coin usage, and referral reward status
  • Referral code, referred user relationship, ad watch count for referral completion, and reward claim status
  • Winning wallet balance, withdrawal request amount, UPI ID, payout details, and withdrawal status
  • Organizer actions needed to manage tournaments, winners, payouts, and fraud prevention
  • Device, app activity, advertising identifiers, and diagnostic data that may be collected by third-party services such as Google AdMob

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, login, and manage user accounts
  • Allow users to earn coins by watching rewarded ads
  • Allow users to join tournaments using app coins
  • Show Free Fire custom room details only to users who joined the tournament
  • Track referral progress when a new user watches the required ads
  • Add referral coins after a referral is completed and claimed
  • Manage winning wallet balances and withdrawal requests
  • Allow the organizer to create tournaments, add room details, approve winners, and review withdrawal requests
  • Prevent fake referrals, duplicate claims, cheating, fraud, and misuse of the app
  • Improve app reliability, safety, and user support

4. Login and Cloud Database

The app uses Supabase for email/password authentication and cloud database storage. Supabase may store account details, profile details, tournament entries, coin balances, referral records, payout details, withdrawal requests, and organizer-managed tournament information.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

5. Ads and Rewards

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and rewarded ads. Users may receive app coins after completing rewarded ads. Google AdMob may collect information such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, app activity, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies.

Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Referral System

Users receive a referral code that can be shared with new users. When a new user signs up with a valid referral code, we store the referral relationship. A referral is completed only after the new user watches the required rewarded ads inside the app. The referrer can then claim referral coins.

Referral data is used only to track referral progress, prevent fake claims, and add referral rewards.

7. Wallet, Payouts, and Withdrawals

The app includes an in-app coin balance and a winning wallet. App coins are used for tournament entry and referral/rewarded ad features. Winning wallet amounts and withdrawal requests are reviewed manually by the organizer.

Users may submit a UPI ID and withdrawal amount for payouts. The app does not automatically transfer money. Users are responsible for entering correct payout details. We are not responsible for failed or incorrect payments caused by incorrect UPI IDs, false information, or third-party payment issues.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. User information may be visible to the organizer for tournament management, winner verification, payout review, fraud prevention, and support.

Information may be processed by services required for app functionality, including Supabase for authentication/database and Google AdMob for ads. We may also disclose information if required by law, platform policy, or to protect users and the app from misuse.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep user data as long as needed to provide app features, manage tournaments, process payouts, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Users may request account deletion or correction of personal information by contacting anupsnaik7349@gmail.com. Some records may be retained when necessary for payment, fraud prevention, legal, or security reasons.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users who are old enough to participate in online gaming tournaments according to local laws, Google Play rules, and game platform rules. If a user is under the required age, they should use the app only with permission from a parent or guardian.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when required by law or platform policies. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last changed.
